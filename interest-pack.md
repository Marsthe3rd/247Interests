## Weighting — read this before drafting, not just the lists below
- General "sharp and interesting" signal still counts on its own — this isn't a pure career newsletter, and good AI/markets/culture coverage is legitimate even with no personal tie-in. But when ranking candidates of similar strength, treat genuine relevance to the two action goals above (the buy-side ops pivot; the brand studio) as a real tiebreaker, not decoration — actively hunt for it rather than waiting for it to surface inside generic AI/markets coverage. That means: ops/middle-office industry structure, hiring and skills demand, automation displacing the exact work he does day to day, buy-side firm economics; and brand/content/identity-system moves, low-capital creative-business plays, platform or distribution economics a solo studio could actually ride.
- Lead-monopoly rule: across any 5-day span, "Market & Tech Moves" should not be led by the same single mega-thread (e.g. the AI/chips buildout story) more than 3 times unless each instance is genuinely new, material information — not a recap of a thread that's gone quiet. If a dominant thread has nothing new, demote it to a short one-liner in Signals and use the lead slot for something else that clears the bar — fashion, e-commerce, psychology, other manufacturing, other startups. One storyline becoming the only thing covered because it's the easiest to find follow-on news for is a failure mode, not thoroughness.
- Length discipline: once a thread has been covered before, the reader already has the mechanism — don't re-teach it from scratch each time. Give the delta (what's new, what it changes) in a few tight sentences; reserve full-length treatment for a genuine first-time deep dive or a true inflection point.

## What counts as signal
- Money mechanics: pricing gaps, supply shifts, new platform economics
- Regulatory or policy changes that open a window
- Tools that collapse a cost I currently pay in time
- Early-stage trends with a monetisation path
- Company/market moves in AI, chips, production/manufacturing, fashion, startups — especially the "why" and "where it's headed", not just the headline
- Culture: brand, campaign, design, ideas, or debates worth studying

## What is noise
- Celebrity, gossip, generic macro commentary
- Anything already mainstream
- "AI is changing everything" think-pieces with no mechanism
- Opportunities needing >£1k upfront
- Platform policy/commission tracking (TikTok Shop/Shopify/Etsy/Instagram rule changes) — retired, not wanted
- Re-explaining a continuing thread's mechanism from scratch when the reader already has it from a prior briefing — give the delta, not the whole recap
