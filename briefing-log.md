Read it as positioning ahead of a mega-valuation IPO, not as a genuine brake being pulled.

## 2026-09-22

### MONEY ON THE MOVE

**1. TikTok Shop US: 30-day affiliate commission protection (live since 14 Sept 2026).**
Mechanism: when TikTok Shop raises a product's affiliate commission rate, creators already promoting it keep that higher rate locked for 30 days even if TikTok cuts it back down afterward — rate increases apply immediately, decreases are delayed for existing promoters. The trade: as an affiliate, jump on a product the moment you see its commission spike, not after — the spike itself buys you a protected 30-day window of the higher rate regardless of what TikTok does next. This is US-only today; TikTok tends to trial seller-economics changes in the US before rolling to UK/EU Shop, so it's worth watching for the same mechanic landing here — when it does, the same "move on the spike, not after" logic applies.
Cost: zero capital, just monitoring + content time. Risk: US-only right now, so not directly usable from Manchester yet; speculative that it rolls to UK.

**2. Platform take-rate is rising while affiliate protection is rising too — a divergence.**
Singapore and EU TikTok Shop commission fees/rates increased in the same window (Singapore adjustment 1 Sept 2026; EU seller commission to 9% from Jan 2026). Mechanism: direct-seller margins on TikTok Shop are being squeezed structurally at the same time creator/affiliate economics are being made *more* predictable and protected. Read: the platform is quietly shifting economic risk from creators onto direct sellers. For someone with more skill/content ability than capital, that's the side of the trade to be on — affiliate/creator, not seller-of-record.
Cost: none. Risk: this is a read on platform incentive design, not a confirmed long-term trend — could reverse.

If nothing else cleared the bar today on money mechanics beyond the above two, that's the honest state — no manufactured third item.
